Daily life

Living

Tree streets, neighborhood schools, a real downtown, and a bus that still goes to Port Authority.

The grain

Most of the houses were here before the interstate.

More than a third of Nutley’s housing predates 1939. Another large share went up in the 1940s–60s for returning veterans, especially in Spring Garden. Colonials, capes, Tudors, and brick two-families under a continuous canopy.

Franklin / Downtown

Around Franklin Avenue: the old Franklinville village, walkable shops, and Memorial Park.

Yanticaw

Northern Nutley near the brook and Route 7. Mid-century houses and exceptional park access.

The Enclosure & Avondale

Architecturally distinct houses tied to the artists’ colony and the old quarry district.

Glendale

Stately lots, colonials, Tudors, and capes under a mature canopy.

Spring Garden

Post-war capes, ranches, and split-levels built for returning veterans.

Kingsland

Around the manor and Kingsland Street (Route 7), near the Clifton line.

Passaic River corridor

Eastern edge along the river: larger lots, more sky, and Booth Park.
